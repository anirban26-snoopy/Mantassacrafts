# Prompt for Google AI Studio — Mantassa Crafts UI/UX Redesign

You are a senior UI/UX designer. Design a modern, high-conversion e-commerce
website UI for a handmade jewelry brand, and produce high-fidelity mockups
(desktop + mobile) plus a short design rationale for each screen.

## Brand

- Name: Mantassa Crafts (মানতাসা ক্রাফটস) — display both English and Bengali
  wherever the logo/name appears.
- Origin: A small, women-owned handmade jewelry studio in Kolkata, India,
  now being revived online with a more modern, professional presence.
- Signature product: hand-painted fabric/terracotta jhumka earrings,
  personalized with the customer's name hand-lettered in Bengali or English
  script, finished with pom-poms and silver jhumka bells. Warm, folk-art,
  handcrafted aesthetic — not gold/diamond luxury.
- Expanding product lines: fine jewelry (metal + stone), watches &
  accessories, and home/gifting keepsakes — all personalizable to some
  degree (color, motif, engraving, or name).
- Tone: warm, personal, artisanal, trustworthy — like buying directly from
  the maker, not a faceless big-box store.
- Social proof: 4.8★ on Google (5 reviews), praised for product quality,
  friendliness, and timely delivery.

## Current visual direction (starting point — feel free to elevate it)

- Palette: warm cream background (#FBF4E8), terracotta/rust primary accent
  (#C1502E), mustard secondary accent (#E0A62F), deep indigo tertiary
  (#2F3B63), warm ink text (#2A2118).
- Typography: Fraunces (serif, display/headings) + Inter (sans, body) +
  Noto Sans Bengali (for Bengali script name previews and the Bengali
  brand name).
- Current state: functional but visually plain — flat cards, placeholder
  color blocks instead of product photography, generic spacing. It needs
  a more considered, premium-but-handmade visual language: think of
  Indian D2C craft brands (e.g. the polish of a modern D2C store) crossed
  with warmth and texture (hand-painted, folk-art, tactile).

## Site structure to design (these pages already exist functionally — you're redesigning the UI/UX, not the information architecture, unless you have a strongly better idea)

1. **Home** — hero (bilingual brand name + tagline), trust bar (rating,
   women-owned, Kolkata-made), shop-by-category grid (4 categories),
   a "Personalize almost anything" feature section, customer
   testimonials (real quotes, first-name attribution), WhatsApp CTA banner.
2. **Shop / category listing** — category hero + product grid, each
   product card shows image, name, starting price, "Personalizable" badge.
3. **Product detail page** — image gallery, price, description, and a
   **live customizer panel**: choose color (swatches), choose motif
   (chips), optional personalized name field with an English/Bengali
   script toggle (live preview of the entered text in the chosen script
   ideally), quantity stepper, "Add to Cart" button.
4. **Cart / checkout page** — cart line items (with chosen color/motif/
   personalization shown per line), quantity controls, running total,
   and a checkout form (full name, phone, email optional, detailed
   address, pincode) that on submit opens WhatsApp with the full order
   pre-filled as a message. No online payment — make this reassuring,
   not confusing (e.g. "We'll confirm your order over WhatsApp — no
   payment needed now").
5. **Customize (how it works)** — a simple numbered-steps explainer
   (pick a piece → choose color/motif → add your name → send on
   WhatsApp → we confirm design & price → handmade & shipped).
6. **About / Our Story** — brand story, values (handmade, personal,
   Kolkata-rooted).
7. **Contact** — WhatsApp CTA, address, embedded map.

## What I need from you

1. A refined **design system**: finalized color palette (with light
   accessible contrast ratios), type scale, spacing scale, button/chip/
   card styles, and how the Bengali script name-personalization feature
   should be visually showcased as the brand's signature moment.
2. High-fidelity **desktop and mobile mockups** (or detailed descriptions
   if you can't render images) for: Home, Product Detail + Customizer,
   and Cart/Checkout — these three screens matter most for conversion.
3. Specific **UX improvements** for the customizer (how to make color/
   motif/name selection feel delightful and reduce hesitation before
   someone commits to a personalized, non-returnable item) and for the
   cart→WhatsApp checkout handoff (how to make a "no online payment,
   confirm via WhatsApp" flow feel trustworthy rather than incomplete).
4. Guidance on using **real product photography** once available
   (crop ratios, background treatment, how to shoot handmade jewelry
   for a cohesive grid) since the current build uses placeholder color
   blocks.
5. Mobile-first considerations, since most customers will likely browse
   and order via phone/WhatsApp.

Keep the aesthetic warm and handmade-feeling, not sterile or corporate —
this is a personal craft brand, not a mass-market jewelry chain.
